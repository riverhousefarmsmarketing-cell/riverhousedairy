# Raw Milk Supply Operation — Homepage Direction
## March 2026

### Core Positioning
"We are building a disciplined, small-herd raw goat and sheep dairy in Washington."

### Business Model (Tiered Distribution)
1. Direct-to-consumer farm pickup (raw goat milk primary)
2. Retail distribution (bottled raw milk)
3. Bulk raw milk supply for local creameries
4. Future value-added products (yogurt, cheese, ice cream)

### Licensing
- WA State raw milk licensing: Application in progress
- Target launch: June 2027 (updated Oct 2026; dairy building shell complete)

### Homepage Structure
1. Hero — Raw goat & sheep milk, location, primary CTA: "Join the Raw Milk List"
2. Our Milk — Goat (year-round) + Sheep (seasonal), factual standards
3. Raw Milk Standards — Clean protocols, no defensive tone
4. What We Raise — Goats primary, sheep secondary
5. Wholesale & Creamery section — Signal capacity and intent
6. Licensing timeline — Calm, factual
7. Value-added products — Phase 2, not distraction
8. Story — Condensed, supports main message
9. Tools — Infrastructure proof, near bottom

### Tone
- Steady, not dramatic
- Professional, not defensive about raw milk
- Measured language: "purposeful breeding" not "rare genetics"
- Signal: process control, operational discipline, regulatory compliance
